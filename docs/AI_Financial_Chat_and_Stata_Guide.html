CFO Macro Shock Monetary Tightening
"Simulate a +200 bps RBI Repo Rate Hike on Highly Indebted Mature Firms and compute ICR compression."

⚡ Simulation Parameters & Financial Findings

  • Policy Shock: $+200\text{ bps}$ benchmark borrowing rate increase (e.g. from $6.50\%$ to $8.50\%$).
  • Sample: Mature manufacturing firms with Debt/Equity $> 0.50$ ($N = 612$ firm-years).
  • Interest Burden Impact: Expands interest expense by $+24.8\%$ YoY.
  • Coverage Compression: Median Interest Coverage Ratio (ICR) compresses from $3.85\times$ down to $2.62\times$.
  • Covenant Breach Risk: Proportion of firms breaching the $1.50\times$ ICR banking covenant jumps from $8.2\%$ to $19.4\%$.
  • Strategic CFO Takeaway: Initiate interest rate swap (IRS) hedges and accelerate short-term debt amortization using surplus operating cash flow.
CFO Operational Event Cost Squeeze
"Simulate a 300 bps raw material margin compression on automotive suppliers and evaluate debt service headroom."

🏭 Margin Squeeze & Cash Flow Findings

  • Operating Margin: Compresses from $16.85\%$ down to $13.85\%$ across auto-component suppliers.
  • Free Cash Flow to Firm (FCFF): Drops by $-28.4\%$, exhausting internal self-financing capacity.
  • Borrowing Headroom: Contracts by $14.2\%$, forcing companies to draw down bank overdrafts to meet working capital requirements.
CFO Working Capital Liquidity Cycle
"Simulate a +30 day elongation in Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) and measure short-term borrowing need."

💧 Working Capital Bottleneck Findings

  • Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC): Extends from 62 days to 92 days due to delayed supply-chain receivables.
  • Liquidity Deficit: Short-term debt borrowing expands by $+18.6\%$ to bridge supplier payables and operational overhead.
  • Mitigation Strategy: Negotiate dynamic discounting with tier-1 OEM customers and implement reverse factoring programs.
CFO Capital Allocation Expansion Financing
"Evaluate capital allocation trade-offs: ₹500 Cr Capex funded by debt vs. retained cash vs. rights issue."

⚖️ Capital Structure Trade-Off Matrix

  • Option A (100% Debt Financed): Leverage increases from $0.28$ to $0.42$; ICR compresses to $2.1\times$; captures interest tax shields but reduces borrowing buffer.
  • Option B (Retained Cash Self-Financed): Preserves borrowing headroom; delays dividend distribution by 24 months (Pecking Order preferred).
  • Option C (Rights Issue): Completely eliminates default risk; dilutes EPS by $8.4\%$.
