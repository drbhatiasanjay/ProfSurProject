# S0 Contract and Policy Workshop Pack

## Purpose and authority

This pack prepares decisions; it does not make them. The recommended options are planning recommendations based on the two pilots. The named accountable roles must approve, reject, or amend them before the dependent stage exits.

## Required participants

- Product owner or sponsor
- Statistical method owner
- Data owner or steward
- Security and privacy owner
- Application/backend lead
- Frontend and accessibility owner
- Evaluation owner
- Operations owner

## Decision sequence

Decisions D-01 through D-05 define the statistical and data boundary. D-06 through D-11 define behavior and evidence contracts. D-12 defines model/data posture. D-13 and D-14 define reference evidence. Later decisions should not be finalized against an unstable upstream contract.

## Workshop exit evidence

S0 exits only when:

1. Each decision has a named approver and recorded disposition.
2. Versioned draft contracts and examples exist for D-01, D-06 through D-11.
3. The variable catalogue interface and ownership are accepted.
4. The capability matrix and statistical policy are accepted for the first slice.
5. Winsorization is explicitly included or excluded.
6. Provider/data posture has separate pilot and production dispositions.
7. Numerical fixtures and tolerance ownership are accepted.
8. Open items are assigned to an explicit later stage gate rather than silently defaulted.

S0 completion permits planning and implementation only for stories whose entry decisions are resolved. It does not establish S1 acceptance, pilot release, production readiness, or runtime correctness.
