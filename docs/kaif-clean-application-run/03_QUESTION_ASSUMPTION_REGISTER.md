# Question and Assumption Register

`UNKNOWN` means no answer was available in tracked ProfSur or the supplied use case. Defaults permit design continuity only; they are not stakeholder-approved facts.

Stage gates:

- **Required before implementation starts** — S0 may resolve the question, but S1 development may not start until the decision is approved.
- **Required before the relevant S1 slice exits** — implementation may begin with the labeled planning default, but the dependent S1 story cannot satisfy Definition of Done until approval.
- **Required before pilot release** — implementation and offline evaluation may proceed, but no researcher pilot may use the affected capability until approval.
- **Required before production release** — controlled work permitted by earlier gates may proceed, but production promotion remains blocked.
