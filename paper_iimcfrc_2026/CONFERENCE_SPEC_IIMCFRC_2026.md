# Conference Submission Specification: IIMCFRC 2026
# IIM Calcutta Finance Research Conference (December 12–14, 2026 | Joka, Kolkata)

---

## 1. Key Deadlines & Details
- **Conference Venue**: IIM Calcutta Campus, Joka, Kolkata, West Bengal, India.
- **Conference Dates**: December 12–14, 2026.
- **Submission Deadline**: September 30, 2026.
- **Notification of Acceptance**: November 15, 2026.
- **Official CFP Portal**: https://www.iimcal.ac.in/events/Call-for-Papers-IIM-Calcutta-Finance-Research-Conference-IIMCFRC-2026

---

## 2. Conference Track Fit
- **Primary Track**: *FinTech, Cryptocurrencies, and Digital Finance / AI & Machine Learning in Finance*
- **Secondary Track**: *Corporate Finance, Governance, and Capital Structure Dynamics*
- **Alternative Track**: *Banking, Financial Intermediation, and Systemic Risk Assessment*

---

## 3. Academic Evaluation Priorities for IIMCFRC
1. **Identification Strategy**: Clear treatment of endogeneity, simultaneity, and unobserved firm heterogeneity.
2. **Methodological Contribution**: Rigorous integration of AI/ML with established econometric models (Double ML, Dynamic Panel GMM).
3. **Emerging Market Relevance**: Demonstrable empirical insights into Indian corporate debt markets under IBC 2016 and RBI prudential regulations.
4. **Reproducibility & Open Science**: Verified reproducible pipeline, clear econometric formulas, and well-defined sample filtering criteria.
